# Automate arithmetic, never adjudication

The app performs calculations whose result is unconditional, and declines every rule that requires a judgement — even where the judgement is easy and the automation would be convenient.

Temporary hit points absorb damage first, automatically, because the rule has no conditions. Death Saves are tapped by hand and never inferred, because whether damage at zero hit points costs one failure or two depends on whether the blow was a critical and whether it landed within five feet — facts the app does not have. Conditions are recorded and change no number. Proficiency in a save or skill is a free toggle with no enforcement of how many the character may have, which also avoids consuming the recursive choice structures rejected in ADR-0004.

## Consequences

Several decisions look inconsistent in isolation and are not: the line is whether a judgement is required, not whether the automation would be nice. Anyone tempted to "finish" one of these — auto-failing a Death Save, applying exhaustion penalties, validating skill selections — is crossing a boundary that was drawn deliberately. The table adjudicates; the app does the sums.
