# Ship SRD 5.1 content only, and defer Homebrew

The published Catalogs contain SRD 5.1 content only, taken from the `5e-bits/5e-database` project under the OGL. Content from other sourcebooks is not shipped, and the unofficial wikis that host it are reproducing copyrighted material we will not redistribute.

## Consequences

SRD 5.1 is thin: nine races, four subraces, twelve classes with exactly **one subclass each**, one background and one feat. Version 1 therefore cannot represent most real characters, and is a foundation for validating the architecture rather than a sheet to bring to a session.

Homebrew is the feature that resolves this — specifically races and subclasses, which are what non-SRD characters actually need. It is deferred, not abandoned; ADR-0009 exists so that deferring it costs nothing later. Homebrew *classes* are not planned even then, because authoring a full Level Table is a large editor for a rare need.

The OGL requires that the license text accompany distributed content, so the app must include it.
